4. **Pro Tips are consistently actionable** — every lesson ends with a genuinely usable tactic.

### Gaps
1. **Zero visuals across lessons.** Every lesson is pure prose. M2 L1 has 13 H3 sections without a single diagram. M2 L2 hides a real formula in prose. Visual craft score: 3-5 range across everything.

3. **Authority claims without specifics** — M2 L1 "I've talked to floor designers…" zero names. M1 L1 mentions $32M once. No specific casino names in first 3 modules. Huge missed brand-authenticity moments.
