## How to use these cards

## The Blackjack Card

**Sit only at a table that passes all three checks:**
1. Pays **3:2** on blackjack, never 6:5. The 6:5 table quietly multiplies the house edge against you. This is printed on the felt.
2. Fewest decks you can find at 3:2 (double deck beats an 8-deck shoe). Ask the dealer the deck count.
3. **No continuous shuffle machine (CSM).** If cards go back in after every hand, walk. You can never get an edge against a CSM.

**Deep penetration wins.** The further the dealer goes before reshuffling, the better. Shallow penetration kills your edge.

**Bankroll fit:** match the table minimum to your bankroll so a normal losing streak cannot bust you in one sitting.

> PULLQUOTE: Never play a 6:5 table. The 3:2 payout is non-negotiable.

## The Baccarat Card

**The only bets worth knowing (lowest house edge first):**
- **Banker:** the lowest edge bet on the table (about 1.06 percent). It wins slightly more than half the decided hands. The 5 percent commission is already priced into that edge.
- **Player:** about 1.24 percent. Fine, slightly worse than Banker.
- **Tie:** avoid. The edge against you is enormous (around 14 percent). The payout never makes up for it.

**Discipline:** baccarat is fast. Set a hand count or a clock and stop on it. Speed is how the small edge grinds bankrolls down.

## The Pai Gow Poker Card

**Why pros like it: it is the slowest bleed in the house.** Most hands push, so your bankroll lasts and your comps clock keeps running.

- Set your hand the **house way** unless you know a better split. The house way is printed and the dealer will set it for you on request.
- **Face-up Pai Gow:** lower edge, more information. Prefer it when available.
- **Bonus / Fortune side bet:** only with a plan and a bankroll for it. The base game is the edge-minimizer; the bonus is variance.
- The goal here is often **break even on the base game while the comps and rebates accrue.**

## The Side Bets Card

**The one that can be worth it:**
- **21+3 (blackjack):** the only side bet on this list that, under the right pay table and conditions, is worth a look. Check the pay table first; they vary a lot.

**Avoid:**
- Insurance (a sucker bet unless you are counting).
- Most carnival and "lucky" side bets. They exist because the edge on them is large.
- Any side bet you cannot explain the pay table for in one sentence.

> PULLQUOTE: A side bet you cannot explain in one sentence is a side bet that is beating you.

## The Bankroll and Session Card

**Set these before you walk in, in writing:**
- **Win limit:** the number where you leave up. Hit it, you leave.
- **Loss limit (stop-loss):** the number where you leave down. Hit it, you leave. No "one more shoe."
- **Session length:** the **30 to 45 minute** window. Play big and fast inside it, then walk. Long sessions feed the house edge.

**Bet sizing:** size so a normal losing run cannot bust your session bankroll. When you do not have an edge, bet the minimum or do not bet. When conditions align, that is when size goes up.

> PULLQUOTE: Play big and fast when it is right, then disappear. The players who stay are the players who give it back.
