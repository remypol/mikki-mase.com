Previous UX audit scored M5 4/10, M6 3/10 — but CONTENT-audit shows Authenticity 8-9 across both. The problem isn't voice, it's **execution + packaging**. Previous scores measured wrong axis.

Every single lesson has a concrete, first-person actionable Pro Tip. These are the highest-value 30 seconds of each lesson. Should be promoted visually.

Every lesson: tables 0, images 0, SVG 0, diagrams 0. Confirmed across 10 lessons. The most egregious:
- M3 L2 "Basic Strategy **Decoded**" with no chart

- M6 L1 seating geometry as prose

Every lesson has 1-3 blockquotes that could each work as a tweet-able Mikki aphorism. These should be the pull-quote moments in the redesign.
