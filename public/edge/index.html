no opinions. no systems. just what each bet keeps, and the math behind it.

the rules these numbers assume
baccarat: 8 decks, tie pays 8:1 unless marked, banker takes 5% commission.
craps field: 2 and 12 pay double unless marked triple.
big six: standard vegas wheel, 54 slots (24/15/7/4/2/1/1), joker and logo pay 40:1.
blackjack: rule set named per row; 'basic strategy' means playing it correctly.
'average, varies' rows are regulator-reported averages, not one machine's number.
every number here is either derived (math shown) or cited (source named). casinos vary. check the felt.
