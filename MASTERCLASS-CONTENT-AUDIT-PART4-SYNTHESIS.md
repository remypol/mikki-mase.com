# SYNTHESIS

## 1. Content DNA (verbatim from auditor)
> "The Mikki Mase Masterclass is primarily an anecdote-forward, mindset-forward course that teaches casino exploitation as a **negotiation and discipline problem rather than a mathematical one**. It centers on three levers — game selection, comp/rebate leverage, and session discipline — and treats each through the lens of 'what the casino does to you' inverted into 'what you do to the casino.' Math is referenced (house edges, payout ratios, rating formulas) but never rendered — no charts, no tables, no worked examples. The voice is consistently declarative, second-person, and confrontational: you are losing, you are wrong, you will lose unless you change. It is closer to a field manual from a practitioner than a textbook from a mathematician."

**Positionering-implicatie:** de huidige marketing zegt "Masterclass" (impliceert video + math rigor). De werkelijke content is een **field manual van een negotiator**. Dit verandert alles over hoe we 't moeten verkopen.

## 5. Missing content (AP curriculum gaps)

1. Hi-Lo card counting (running/true count, bet spreads, drills)
2. KO Knock-Out count
3. Wonging — table entry/exit on count
4. Hole-card play
5. Shuffle tracking
6. **Kelly Criterion + fractional Kelly bet sizing** (should anchor bankroll talk)
7. **Risk of ruin calculations**
8. **Property-by-property blackjack rule matrices** (3:2 vs 6:5, S17 vs H17, DAS, surrender, pen — Strip + regional)
9. **Tax implications** (W-2G, session accounting, pro-gambler status, CTR/SAR)
10. **Video poker advantage play** (full-pay Jacks or Better, Deuces Wild — best-EV game category, completely absent)

Intentional omissions vs gaps: counting/wonging/hole-card absence feels intentional (Mikki positions away from "counter" stereotype). **Kelly / risk-of-ruin / rule matrices / tax content are GAPS** — belong in this course and aren't there.

## 10. Top 10 redesign hit-list (ranked by leverage)

1. **Ship a real basic strategy chart + trainer on M3 L2.** Single biggest embarrassment. Flagship "Basic Strategy Decoded" with zero chart is indefensible. Highest leverage per engineering hour.

2. **Tables on every lesson that cites numbers.** 7 lessons identified. Cheap to ship, triples retention.

3. **Wire Casino IQ as pre/post + per-module diagnostic.** Gate module completion on "improved since pre-test" rather than "clicked Mark Complete."

4. **One interactive widget per module.** Start: M3 basic strategy trainer · M8 rebate calculator · M10 session timer. All three usable outside the course → driver of retention + word-of-mouth.

5. **Cut or rewrite the 5 generic lessons.** Inject Mikki voice + specificity, or delete.

6. **Add counting / wonging / video poker modules.** Closes the "is this actually advantage play?" credibility gap. Even one "Counting 101" would legitimize as full AP curriculum.

7. **Quiz back-links to source lesson sections.** 2 lines of JavaScript.

8. **Show pass threshold + retake policy + question shuffling on quizzes.**

9. **Cross-module callouts** in UI. Material already builds; UI doesn't show it.

10. **Unlock the 2 gated surfaces for Masterclass tier OR rename the tier.** Daily Drops + AI Advisor redirect a paid-course user to "Upgrade to Inner Circle" banner — biggest trust-breaker in the product.
